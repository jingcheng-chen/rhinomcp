Matches across views

Compare silhouettes and agreed landmarks under matched cameras. Check seat/back proportions, tilt, frame profile and cushion layout. Reserve views that the modeler never sees.

Thresholds to calibrate
02 / Is a useful model

Valid, organized geometry

Open the saved .3dm independently. Check named parts, intended solids/surfaces, thickness and symmetry, and unwanted intersections. Agree the required editability before scoring.

Part rules to define
03 / Actually improves

Beats the starting point

Keep screenshots, evaluator and agent version fixed. Compare before and after across repeated trials and unseen cases, including cost and tool calls. Preserve earlier passing tasks.

Comparison to implement

Automatic scoring is feasible once views and rules are fixed. Screenshots alone do not reveal exact scale or all hidden surfaces. Use one known dimension, or evaluate up to a common scale; record assumptions for unseen geometry. Human judgment helps define and calibrate visual quality before routine scoring becomes automatic.
