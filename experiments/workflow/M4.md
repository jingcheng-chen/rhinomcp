[The public ledger](held-out-bank.json) records family exclusions, novelty reviews,

missing private bundles are reported unavailable, not silently reconstructed.

## Allocation behavior verified

The supervisor CLI writes an atomic, hash-chained ledger under a writer lock.
It spends the whole family before releasing parameters, binds it to one validation
and the predeclared review hash, and forbids reuse even after a failed or abandoned
attempt. Failed exports remain spent; an unchanged export can be recovered only
for the same open validation. Closing any outcome requires a sealed replacement.
Lost, changed or leaked bundles can be retired without exporting them.

Fourteen synthetic lifecycle tests exercise content/history tampering, missing
private files, duplicate families, discovery overlap, duplicate validation
allocation, export failure and recovery, all four closure outcomes, replacement
requirements, invalid parameters, path traversal, writer contention and retirement.
The real bank was verified without disclosing its parameter values in the report.
The two M3 historical spends are imported with their original task/result hashes,
then replenished and closed; no pre-M3 cryptographic commitment is invented.

This is a supervised workflow boundary. File permissions, role tool scopes, hashes
and Git history help preserve custody and provenance; they are not an adversarial
OS sandbox. Semantic novelty is independently reviewed rather than inferred from
an identifier. Future comparisons must use the shared runner, calibrate against the
sealed requirements and freeze their own inputs before modeling. The bank does
not dispatch agents or automatically install/promote candidates.
